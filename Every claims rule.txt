Every claim in the project must belong to one of these three categories:

Level 1 — Observed

Directly measurable from the raw data.

Example:

Dataset A contains 677,437 rows.

No citation needed.

Level 2 — Derived

Calculated by our pipeline.

Example:

165,393 overlapping RSIDs.

Must be reproducible by our code.

Level 3 — External Knowledge

Comes from biology literature or reference databases.

Example:

rs12913832 is associated with eye color.

Must have a citation (e.g., HirisPlex-S paper, dbSNP, ClinVar, or another peer-reviewed source).
